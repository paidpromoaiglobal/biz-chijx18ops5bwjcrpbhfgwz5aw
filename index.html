About Us

Inkthology Tattoo Studio

Inkthology Tattoo Studio is Guwahati's trusted destination for fine tattoo artistry, nestled near Netaji Statue at Pandu College Gate, Maligaon. With a stellar 4.6-star rating and a growing community of satisfied clients, we bring your most personal visions to life through skilled hands and a passion for the craft. Whether you're getting your first tattoo or adding to an existing collection, our studio offers a clean, welcoming, and professional environment you can trust.

At Inkthology, we believe every tattoo tells a unique story. Our artists combine traditional techniques with contemporary styles to deliver custom designs tailored to your personality and preferences. From minimalist line work to bold tribal pieces, from delicate portraits to intricate geometric patterns — we do it all with care, hygiene, and artistic excellence. Open seven days a week, we're here to make your tattoo journey safe, memorable, and truly one of a kind.
